## What this run taught
The first blind build passed every one of the 452 cases in four minutes. Everything it found
came from its notes, not from the suite. Three of its choices were readings the spec allowed
but did not state: which values an operation canonicalizes, whether "has exactly one of" means
present or valid, and whether a polygon with one bad point is still a polygon. One of its
"already pinned" choices exposed a bug in the suite rather than the spec. It looked up members
as own properties, so `constructor` counts as an unknown member. The suite's model used a
JavaScript `in` check and called such an envelope valid. The builder knew a trap that the
author of the suite fell into.
